This is the privacy policy for You Cooked It (youcooked-it.com), operated by Croft & Hugh. We keep it simple, because we collect almost nothing.

No cookies from us. We don't set tracking cookies, show personalised ads, or follow you around the internet. That's why there's no cookie banner here.

Anonymous analytics. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) to count visits and see which recipes people love. It doesn't identify you and doesn't track you across sites.

Third parties. Our fonts load from Google Fonts and the site is hosted on Vercel, so those services see standard technical data (like your IP address) needed to deliver the page. Some outbound links may be affiliate links, see our affiliate disclosure.

If we ever add anything that changes this, like an email newsletter, we'll ask you first and explain it there, in plain English.
