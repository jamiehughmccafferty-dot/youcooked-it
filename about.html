you cooked it

You Cooked It is an independent kitchen project from Croft & Hugh. We got tired of recipe sites that bury the food under pop-ups, life stories and fifteen ads, so we built the opposite: one recipe, one screen, and a steady hand on your shoulder from "prepping it" to "you cooked it."

Every recipe is written for real UK home kitchens, with metric measures, supermarket ingredients and honest timings, then shaped into a cook-along you can actually follow with floury hands: a live serving scaler, step timers, and a little celebration when you're done. Because you did cook it.

We're a small operation and we're building the biggest, calmest recipe database in the UK, one lane at a time. If a recipe let you down or you want us to cook something next, we want to hear about it.
